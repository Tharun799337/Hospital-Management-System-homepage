Free Cancellation (24+ hours)

Cancel or reschedule 24+ hours before your appointment at no charge. Full refund within 3–5 business days.

Late Cancellation (4–24 hours)

Cancellations between 4 and 24 hours before incur a 20% fee. Rescheduling is always free in this window.

No-Show / Same Day (<4 hours)

Cancellations within 4 hours or no-shows are charged 50% of the consultation fee. Medical emergencies are excepted.

Rescheduling is always free with 4+ hours notice. Use your Appointment ID online or call us.
